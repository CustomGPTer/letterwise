Every word. Every day.

Letterwise is a word-guessing game for Android. Find the hidden word in six tries: green means the right letter in the right place, yellow means it is in the word somewhere else. Play today's Daily Puzzle, then carry on with as many words as you like.

It is free, with no adverts and nothing to unlock. Tens of thousands of words are built in, so it works on a plane, underground or anywhere with no signal.

How it works

No sign-up and no permissions to start. Type your name, pick a character and play.

  1. Guess a word

    Type any real word and press Enter. Each letter turns green, yellow or grey to show how close you are.

  2. Use the colours

    Keep the green letters, move the yellow ones, and drop the grey. Six tries for five letters, more for longer words.

  3. Come back tomorrow

    A new Daily Puzzle arrives at midnight, the same word on every phone. In between, play as many words as you like.

Installing and updating

Letterwise is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called Letterwise.apk and is about 6.3 MB. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps. Allow it, go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

  3. Updating later

    In Letterwise, open Settings and tap Check for updates. It asks GitHub for the newest version, downloads it if there is one, checks it is genuine, and Android asks you to confirm. Your players, games and stats are kept.

Questions

Is Letterwise really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no coins and no adverts. Every mode, sticker and badge is in the free app.

Is it on Google Play?

No. Letterwise is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 8 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. All 25,052 answers, the guess list and every meaning are built into the app, so it plays in flight mode. Only Check for updates goes online, and only when you tap it.

Is it the same word for everyone each day?

The Daily Puzzle is the same on every phone running Letterwise, worked out from the date. Puzzle #1 was on 22 November 2026, and a new one arrives at midnight.

How many words are there?

25,052 answers from three to eight letters (Classic uses four to seven), in three levels: Easy, Normal and Expert. Many more words are accepted as guesses, in UK and US spelling.

How do two phones play with no internet?

Both phones open Two Players and Race on two phones. One starts a game and the other joins it from the list or by scanning its QR code. They talk to each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, so it works in flight mode with Bluetooth switched on.

How do I give a friend the app?

Open Two Players and Give a friend the app. Send it by Bluetooth or Quick Share, let them download it from your phone over the same Wi-Fi or your hotspot, or show them the QR code for this website.

Can several people use one phone?

Yes. Up to eight players, each with their own words, streaks, stats, stickers and badges. Tap your character at the top of Home to switch.

What does the widget show?

Today's Daily Puzzle number, whether you have played it, your colours and your streak. It changes to the new puzzle at midnight without opening the app. Tap it to play.

Can I keep my games if I change phone?

Yes. Settings, Back up to a file saves everything to one file you can keep, for example in Google Drive. On the new phone, Restore from a file puts it all back.

How do I get new versions?

Open Settings and tap Check for updates. Letterwise asks GitHub, downloads the new version if there is one, checks it is Letterwise signed by the same maker, and Android asks you to confirm. Your games are kept.

Who made it?

Letterwise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. It is not connected with any other word game or its owners.
