Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Letterwise app for Android (package uk.jj.letterwise) and this website. Letterwise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing from the app.
  • The game never goes online. Every word and meaning is built in.
  • Check for updates contacts GitHub, only when you tap it.
  • Your players, games and stats stay on your phone, and phone backups leave them out.

Who the app talks to, and when

WhoWhat it receivesWhen
GitHubYour IP address and a request for the newest Letterwise releaseOnly when you tap Check for updates, and when you choose to download an update
A friend's phone nearbyThe name, character and colour you play as, the word code, and the colours of each guess (not the letters until the round ends)Only during a two-phone race you start or join
A friend on the same Wi-FiThe Letterwise app file, from your phoneOnly while the Give a friend the app screen is open

A two-phone race goes directly between the two phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, using Google Play services on each phone to make the connection. There is no Letterwise server, so nothing is sent to the developer.

What stays on your phone

Your players, games, results, stickers and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for Letterwise. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

What the app can reach

Nearby devices, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (only for a two-phone race, asked for when you first start one), the camera (only to scan a QR code, when you tap Scan), the internet (only for Check for updates and for sending the app to a friend over local Wi-Fi), vibration, and installing packages (only to hand a downloaded, verified Letterwise update to Android, which asks you to confirm). No location, contacts, microphone or storage access.

Backups and sharing

Back up to a file saves your players, games and stats where you choose. Share sends a spoiler-free grid of coloured squares through your phone's share menu. Neither happens unless you ask.

Deleting your data

Settings, Clear stats removes a player's games and tallies, and removing a player removes everything about them. Uninstalling Letterwise removes everything. Neither can recall a backup file you saved somewhere else.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Letterwise collects nothing from anyone. Offensive words are kept out of the answers, and nothing in it is unsuitable for children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 22 November 2026.
