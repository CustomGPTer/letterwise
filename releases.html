Release notes

What changed in each version of Letterwise. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First public release. 25,052 answers from three to eight letters, each with its meaning, in UK spelling with US spellings accepted as guesses.
  • Daily Puzzle: one five-letter everyday word a day, the same on every phone, with streaks and a spoiler-free share grid.
  • Classic with 4 to 7 letters, Easy, Normal and Expert words, Hard mode, hints, and Practice where nothing counts.
  • Word Fever, Secret Word and Quad, each with its own scores and lengths to open.
  • Two players: pass the phone, race on two phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct with no internet, or set a friend a word by QR code.
  • Up to eight players with their own characters, words and stats, a podium for this phone and a head-to-head tally against each opponent.
  • 144 stickers in 12 albums and 30 badges, all earned by playing.
  • A home-screen widget for the Daily Puzzle, back up and restore, sending the app to a friend's phone, Check for updates and More apps.
  • Light, dark and follow-system themes, colour-blind colours, high contrast and reduce motion. The opening page can be switched off.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Letterwise.apk. It is 6.3 MB (6,627,584 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

051c980fbdf08dca375c8bcf5201ff97e87c6bbfb4257dd5597cf067a30ca710

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Letterwise.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
