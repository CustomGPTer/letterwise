What Letterwise does not do

Letterwise is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and update it from inside the app. It has no online leaderboards or play against strangers: two-player games are with someone nearby, or by sharing a word code.

Where the others do more

NYT Games has the original Wordle with WordleBot, plus the Crossword, Spelling Bee, Connections and more, with friend leaderboards. Wordle! by Lion Studios Plus has seasonal events and online matches. Both are on Google Play.

About the names

Letterwise is an independent game and is not connected with Wordle, The New York Times or Lion Studios. Wordle is a trademark of The New York Times Company.
