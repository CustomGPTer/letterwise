**First public release.** A free word-guessing game for Android with 25,052 answers built in. No adverts, no account, and no internet needed to play.

- **Daily Puzzle:** one five-letter everyday word a day, the same on every phone, with streaks and a spoiler-free share grid.
- **Classic:** 4 to 7 letters, Easy, Normal or Expert words, Hard mode and hints. **Practice** where nothing counts.
- **Word Fever, Secret Word and Quad.**
- **Two players:** pass the phone, race on two phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct with no internet, or set a friend a word by QR code. Head-to-head tallies.
- Up to eight players, 144 stickers, 30 badges, a home-screen widget, backup and restore, sending the app to a friend's phone, Check for updates and More apps.

Android 8 or later. Install: download Letterwise.apk on your phone and open it. Updates: Settings, Check for updates.
